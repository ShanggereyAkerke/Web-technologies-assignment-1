Good to know.

How do I choose a size?

Measure your chest, waist and hips, then compare them with our size guide. Measurements are examples for this student project.

Can I place an order?

FORYOU is a student concept. The prices and products illustrate the design; orders and payments are not accepted.

Will this form send a message?

No. You can explore the fields, but there is no message service. The send button is disabled.
